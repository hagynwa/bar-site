# BRIEF · עוד קצת ילדות (anidoodle)

What the person said is marked (them). Everything else is my decision (mine) and can be vetoed in one line.

- **Kind**: a series of 8 short explainers, one research fact each (them). Hebrew, right to left (them).
- **Medium**: drawn, 2D (mine: the marks are the point; a child's handmade page).
- **Style**: a combination of "the notebook" and "the yard" (them). Built as one medium (mine):
  a squared maths exercise book with a red margin on the right, marker lettering (Playpen Sans Hebrew),
  wax-crayon highlighter and doodles (after the balloon plate), and torn/cut construction-paper collage
  of the logo's world, hills, sun, house, lollipop trees, glued and taped onto the page (after the fox plate).
- **The visual rule** (mine): screens are always cold and dark or screen-blue; childhood is colour, paper and crayon.
- **Palette**: the parents' leadership logo (them, logo supplied): navy #001A3F, green #699F03, leaf #B5D96E,
  sun #FECC0C, page yellow #FEE55A, heart red #F61621, purple #CC90CE, sky #3ABCFE.
- **Shape**: 9x16 for Reels/TikTok/WhatsApp status (them: vertical, short), plus 1x1 for Facebook (mine).
- **Length**: 25 to 40 s per episode (them: short and focused).
- **Hook**: the first 2 seconds stop the scroll, before any branding (them).
- **Open and close**: identical in every episode (them). Opening sting about 1 s, after the hook (mine).
- **Narration**: Bar Shalev, recorded voice (them). Captions burned in (mine: most viewing is silent).
- **Music**: one light theme with variations (them: suitable music). Style to be proposed at the script stage; ask before any score work.
- **Facts**: every claim sourced in facts.json, source shown on screen (them).
- **fps**: 30 (mine). Paper moves on twos (12 fps feel) where it is laid down, like stop-motion (mine).

## Look still (approval gate 1)

`node tools/still.mjs yaldut --frame 0 --out out/look-hook.png` · reproducible.
Episode 1 hook: "איפה הטלפון של הילד שלך ישן בלילה?" over a night yard: the house is asleep, one window glows screen-blue.

Critique (mine):
- Works: the grid page and margin read as a school notebook at a glance; the marker hand is legible at phone size;
  the crayon highlighter carries the key words; torn edges show the white core; tape sells "glued in";
  the single blue window is the focal point and carries the campaign's idea.
- To improve in production: the house reads flat (lay a darker paper on the shade side); the sky's dye blotches
  are a little strong; the rays should be drawn on, one by one, as the hook's motion.
